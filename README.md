Hi, I'm Tri (or you can call me Kris). I'm a Data Science major based in Ho Chi Minh City.
## My notable project

**Urban tree species classification** ([Urban-Tree-Classification](https://github.com/KrisEvK/Urban-Tree-Classification)): a Streamlit app that identifies 19 street tree species in Ben Nghe Ward from a leaf photo. Found a train/test leak, fixed it, and compared five vision models. A two-stage cascade ensemble reached 98.35% accuracy and runs in about 20 ms per image.

**VInFi-Check** ([VInfi-Check](https://github.com/KrisEvK/VInfi-Check)): a Vietnamese version of InFi-Check that detects hallucinations in LLM summaries across six error types. A dataset of about 4,800 samples and fine-tuned Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct with QLoRA on a Colab T4.

**Macroeconomic data warehouse** ([macro-data-warehouse](https://github.com/KrisEvK/macro-data-warehouse)): 18 indicators for 150+ countries from 2017 to 2023, modelled as a star schema with one fact table and eight dimensions. The ETL is built with SSIS, with an SSAS cube queried through MDX, a Flask explorer service, and embedded Power BI dashboards.

**CSSI, a stress index for Vietnamese commodities** ([vietnam-commodity-stress-index](https://github.com/KrisEvK/vietnam-commodity-stress-index)): a two-layer PCA and a 3-state Gaussian HMM that flag stress regimes in five commodity groups using monthly data from 2003 to 2024. Granger tests show Beverages stress leading Coffee and Energy by one to three months.
